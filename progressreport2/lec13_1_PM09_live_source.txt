PM09. Cobblevane Realty is made up; the places and the public data services below are real. With this sheet your team gives its agent a live data connection for the items marked LIVE DATA in your progress report 2 questions: 1.3 (mortgage rates). Every box marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


THE TOOL, get_mortgage_rates

Open your team's agent, click Add Knowledge Source and choose API Call. Copy each field below into the field of the same name, choose the Method GET, and click Add. Then save the agent.

Name, COPY:
get_mortgage_rates
Description, COPY:
Use when the team asks about mortgage rates. Returns the average 30 year fixed mortgage rate in the United States, in percent, week by week since January 2026, from Freddie Mac's survey as published by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, the latest week last.
Parameter Schema, COPY:
{"type":"object","properties":{}}
Endpoint, COPY:
https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=MORTGAGE30US&cosd=2026-01-01

WHAT COMES BACK

The response is comma separated text: a first row with the column names, observation_date and MORTGAGE30US, then one row per week, the oldest first and the latest last. Each value is the average 30 year fixed mortgage rate in percent for the week ending on that date.

To see what the agent receives, open the Endpoint in a browser.

TEST IT

Start a new thread with your agent and send this question. Then open Execution Details and look at Tool Calls, and compare the answer with the response in your browser.
COPY: What is the latest 30 year mortgage rate, and for which week?


NOTE

The rate comes from Freddie Mac's weekly survey of lenders, published every Thursday. Read the direction of the change over the last few weeks, and say so when you report it.
