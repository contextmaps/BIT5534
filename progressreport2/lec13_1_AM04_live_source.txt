THE TOOL, get_forecast

Open your team's agent, click Add Knowledge Source and choose API Call. Copy each field below into the field of the same name, choose the Method GET, and click Add. Then save the agent.

Name, COPY:
get_forecast
Description, COPY:
Use when the team asks about tomorrow's weather at the airports. Returns the daily forecast for the next 3 days at Charlotte, Washington Dulles, Roanoke, Charlottesville, Asheville, Charleston WV, Tri-Cities TN, Lynchburg, Greenville SC and Morgantown, in that order: weather code, chance of rain or snow, snowfall in inches and strongest wind gust in miles per hour.
Parameter Schema, COPY:
{"type":"object","properties":{}}
Endpoint, COPY:
https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast?latitude=35.21,38.95,37.33,38.14,35.44,38.37,36.48,37.33,34.90,39.64&longitude=-80.94,-77.46,-79.98,-78.45,-82.54,-81.59,-82.41,-79.20,-82.22,-79.92&daily=weather_code,precipitation_probability_max,snowfall_sum,wind_gusts_10m_max&temperature_unit=fahrenheit&precipitation_unit=inch&wind_speed_unit=mph&timezone=America%2FNew_York&forecast_days=3

The places, in the order of the response: Charlotte; Washington Dulles; Roanoke; Charlottesville; Asheville; Charleston WV; Tri-Cities TN; Lynchburg; Greenville SC; Morgantown.

WHAT COMES BACK

The response is in JSON. For several places it is a list, one entry per place in the order above; for one place it is a single entry. In each entry, daily.time lists the dates, and each other field under daily lists one value per date, in the same order. daily_units gives the unit of each field. Weather codes: 0 clear, 1 to 3 partly cloudy to overcast, 45 and 48 fog, 51 to 67 drizzle and rain, 71 to 77 snow, 80 to 82 rain showers, 95 to 99 thunderstorms.

To see what the agent receives, open the Endpoint in a browser.

TEST IT
