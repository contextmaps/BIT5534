KETTLE RIDGE MUNICIPAL ELECTRIC, LIVE SOURCE SHEET
PM07. Kettle Ridge Municipal Electric is made up; the places and the public data services below are real. With this sheet your team gives its agent a live data connection for the items marked LIVE DATA in your progress report 2 questions: 1.3 (the coming week). Every box marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


THE TOOL, get_forecast

Open your team's agent, click Add Knowledge Source and choose API Call. Copy each field below into the field of the same name, choose the Method GET, and click Add. Then save the agent.

Name, COPY:
get_forecast
Description, COPY:
Use when the team asks about the coming week's temperatures in Kettle Ridge. Returns the daily forecast for the next 7 days: low, high and mean temperature in Fahrenheit. A day's heating degree days are 65 minus its mean temperature, and its cooling degree days its mean minus 65, when positive.
Parameter Schema, COPY:
{"type":"object","properties":{}}
Endpoint, COPY:
https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast?latitude=37.23&longitude=-80.41&daily=temperature_2m_min,temperature_2m_max,temperature_2m_mean&temperature_unit=fahrenheit&timezone=America%2FNew_York&forecast_days=7

The places, in the order of the response: Kettle Ridge.

WHAT COMES BACK

The response is in JSON. For several places it is a list, one entry per place in the order above; for one place it is a single entry. In each entry, daily.time lists the dates, and each other field under daily lists one value per date, in the same order. daily_units gives the unit of each field. Weather codes: 0 clear, 1 to 3 partly cloudy to overcast, 45 and 48 fog, 51 to 67 drizzle and rain, 71 to 77 snow, 80 to 82 rain showers, 95 to 99 thunderstorms.

To see what the agent receives, open the Endpoint in a browser.

TEST IT

Start a new thread with your agent and send this question. Then open Execution Details and look at Tool Calls, and compare the answer with the response in your browser.

NOTE

Kettle Ridge is made up, so its forecast uses the coordinates of Blacksburg, Virginia, a real town in mountains with cold winters and hot summers.
