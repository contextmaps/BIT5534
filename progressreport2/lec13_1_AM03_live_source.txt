THE TOOL, get_food_prices

Open your team's agent, click Add Knowledge Source and choose API Call. Copy each field below into the field of the same name, choose the Method GET, and click Add. Then save the agent.

Name, COPY:
get_food_prices
Description, COPY:
Use when the team asks about grocery prices. Returns the consumer price index for food at home in United States cities, from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, month by month for recent years, the latest month first.
Parameter Schema, COPY:
{"type":"object","properties":{}}
Endpoint, COPY:
https://api.bls.gov/publicAPI/v2/timeseries/data/CUUR0000SAF11

WHAT COMES BACK

The response is in JSON. Under Results, series, data, each entry is one month, the latest first: year, periodName (the month) and value. The index is 100 in 1982 to 1984, so the change in percent between two months is the newer value divided by the older one, minus 1. The national unemployment rate is a percent, not an index.

To see what the agent receives, open the Endpoint in a browser.

TEST IT

Start a new thread with your agent and send this question. Then open Execution Details and look at Tool Calls, and compare the answer with the response in your browser.
COPY: What is the latest value of the food at home index, and for which month?


NOTE

The Bureau of Labor Statistics allows 25 calls a day without a key, and every Hokie AI agent calls from the same server, so ask the agent for the index once and reuse the answer within a session. If your team wants more, a member can register a free key at data.bls.gov/registrationEngine and add ?registrationkey= followed by the key right after the Endpoint, with nothing else changed.
