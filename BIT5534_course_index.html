BIT 5534
Applied Business Intelligence & Analytics

A thread-centered, fifteen-week graduate course in which every analytics method is specified in plain language, built as a working tool with an AI assistant, verified against something you can check, and read for what it means for a decision.

Course Index

Foundations

Structure & Segmentation

Finding the shape of data without a target variable: components, distances, and segments you can defend.

Weeks 4–5 · 4 lectures

Phase III

Prediction & Comparison

Trees, forests, networks, margins, and ensembles, then an honest harness that scores them all the same way.

Weeks 6–11 · 12 lectures

Phase IV

Project & Delivery

Team-built business intelligence and analytics dashboards, developed in class and presented to peers.
