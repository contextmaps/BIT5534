BRIGHTRAFTER SOLAR, GOVERNANCE SHEET
PM04. Brightrafter Solar is made up. With this sheet your team takes its recommendation with the most at stake through the five governance gates your instructor showed on Lumenvale, with one question at each gate. Your team uses the answers as material for the governance box of its progress report 2 team thread, which your team writes in its own words. About twenty five minutes, with the model run.


YOUR BUSINESS AT THE GATES

Who decides: The sales manager, who decides which leads receive a home visit and how fast new leads are called.
Who is affected: Homeowners.
Your live tool: get_sunlight, from Open-Meteo, the sunlight forecast at each county seat for the next 7 days. The forecast covers 7 days of sunlight at one point in each county, and the shade on a single roof is outside what a county forecast measures.


THE RECOMMENDATION

Your team's recommendation for the gates is to send home visits to the leads with the highest chance of signing. It belongs to the decision on the home visit in your brief. If your team's synthesis has a recommendation with more at stake, your team may use it instead, with the report behind it, and change the first message below to match.


RUN THE MODEL

Open the REG tool from the analytics workbench, load brightrafter_leads.csv, and fit this run. Then press Save report and keep the .txt file.

Target: signed
Validation: the Validation column
Inputs: county, lead_source, monthly_bill_usd, roof_area_sqft, roof_pitch, roof_facing, shading_pct, home_value_usd, credit_band and days_to_first_call
Leave out: site_survey_scheduled, because it is recorded after the outcome; call_notes, a text column; quoted_value_usd, the other target; and every other column of the file, such as the id and date columns.


THE FIRST MESSAGE

Start a new thread with your team's agent, attach the .txt report, and send the message below.

COPY:
Our recommendation is to send home visits to the leads with the highest chance of signing. It belongs to the decision on the home visit. The attached report is a REG fit of signed, the report the recommendation rests on. I will ask you one question about it at each of five governance gates.


GATE 1, LIVE DATA

COPY:
Call get_sunlight and say which of its values bear on this recommendation, each with its date. What should the sales manager do if the tool fails?

Then open Execution Details and look at Tool Calls. Check that the agent called get_sunlight and that each value in its answer matches the tool's response.


GATE 2, EVIDENCE

COPY:
Which numbers in the attached report support this recommendation, and how far can the sales manager trust them? Name the validation rows behind them and any input recorded after the outcome.


GATE 3, FAIRNESS AND PRIVACY

COPY:
Under this recommendation, some homeowners get a visit or a faster call and others do not. Could county, home_value_usd or credit_band stand in for income, age or race, and does acting on it use what call_notes say about a homeowner in a way the homeowner would not expect?


GATE 4, AUTHORITY

COPY:
Which part of this recommendation is yours, and which part does the sales manager decide? Does any part go beyond the conclusions our brief lists under CONCLUSIONS THE AGENT MAY NOT DRAW?


GATE 5, MONITORING

COPY:
If the sales manager acts on this recommendation, which number would show that it worked, which number would show that it went wrong, and when should we look again? How would we notice if get_sunlight stopped working?


THEN READ THE ANSWERS

Read the five answers with the box "Reading the answers, gate by gate" on the lecture page, and decide which gate needs the most work. That gate is the main risk of your recommendation, and what would make it pass is your safeguard. Write your team's governance box in your own words.
