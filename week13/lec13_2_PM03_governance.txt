TASKMEADOW, GOVERNANCE SHEET
PM03. Taskmeadow is made up. With this sheet your team takes its recommendation with the most at stake through the five governance gates your instructor showed on Lumenvale, with one question at each gate. Your team uses the answers as material for the governance box of its progress report 2 team thread, which your team writes in its own words. About twenty five minutes, with the model run.


YOUR BUSINESS AT THE GATES

Who decides: The trust and safety manager, who sets the escrow rules and decides which projects get an early check.
Who is affected: Clients and freelancers.
Your live tool: get_rates, from Frankfurter, the exchange rates published by the European Central Bank. The European Central Bank publishes rates on working days only, so on a weekend or a holiday the latest rates are the last working day's.


THE RECOMMENDATION

Your team's recommendation for the gates is to hold more of the payment in escrow on the projects with the highest chance of a dispute. It belongs to the decision on the escrow hold in your brief. If your team's synthesis has a recommendation with more at stake, your team may use it instead, with the report behind it, and change the first message below to match.


RUN THE MODEL

Open the REG tool from the analytics workbench, load taskmeadow_projects.csv, and fit this run. Then press Save report and keep the .txt file.

Target: dispute
Validation: the Validation column
Inputs: category, budget_usd, deadline_days, freelancer_country, cross_border, payment_method, freelancer_rating, freelancer_past_projects, client_past_disputes and revisions_requested
Leave out: support_tickets_after_delivery, because it is recorded after the outcome; client_brief, a text column; payout_usd, the other target; and every other column of the file, such as the id and date columns.


THE FIRST MESSAGE

Start a new thread with your team's agent, attach the .txt report, and send the message below.

COPY:
Our recommendation is to hold more of the payment in escrow on the projects with the highest chance of a dispute. It belongs to the decision on the escrow hold. The attached report is a REG fit of dispute, the report the recommendation rests on. I will ask you one question about it at each of five governance gates.


GATE 1, LIVE DATA

COPY:
Call get_rates and say which of its values bear on this recommendation, each with its date. What should the trust and safety manager do if the tool fails?

Then open Execution Details and look at Tool Calls. Check that the agent called get_rates and that each value in its answer matches the tool's response.


GATE 2, EVIDENCE

COPY:
Which numbers in the attached report support this recommendation, and how far can the trust and safety manager trust them? Name the validation rows behind them and any input recorded after the outcome.


GATE 3, FAIRNESS AND PRIVACY

COPY:
Under this recommendation, some payments are held longer or some projects are checked more closely. Could freelancer_country or cross_border stand in for nationality, so that people are judged by where they live, and does acting on it use what client_brief says in a way the client would not expect?


GATE 4, AUTHORITY

COPY:
Which part of this recommendation is yours, and which part does the trust and safety manager decide? Does any part go beyond the conclusions our brief lists under CONCLUSIONS THE AGENT MAY NOT DRAW?


GATE 5, MONITORING

COPY:
If the trust and safety manager acts on this recommendation, which number would show that it worked, which number would show that it went wrong, and when should we look again? How would we notice if get_rates stopped working?


THEN READ THE ANSWERS

Read the five answers with the box "Reading the answers, gate by gate" on the lecture page, and decide which gate needs the most work. That gate is the main risk of your recommendation, and what would make it pass is your safeguard. Write your team's governance box in your own words.
