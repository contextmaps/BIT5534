QUARRYSTEP RESIDENTIAL, GOVERNANCE SHEET
PM01. Quarrystep Residential is made up. With this sheet your team takes its recommendation with the most at stake through the five governance gates your instructor showed on Lumenvale, with one question at each gate. Your team uses the answers as material for the governance box of its progress report 2 team thread, which your team writes in its own words. About twenty five minutes, with the model run.


YOUR BUSINESS AT THE GATES

Who decides: The community managers, who set asking rents and concessions, and the regional director, who sets the renewal policy.
Who is affected: Tenants and the people looking for an apartment.
Your live tool: get_rent_index, from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the consumer price index for rent, month by month. BLS answers 25 calls a day without a key, publishes each month about a month later, and reports rents for United States cities as a whole.


THE RECOMMENDATION

Your team's recommendation for the gates is to offer a concession on the units with the highest chance of sitting empty for 45 days. It belongs to the decision on the concession in your brief. If your team's synthesis has a recommendation with more at stake, your team may use it instead, with the report behind it, and change the first message below to match.


RUN THE MODEL

Open the REG tool from the analytics workbench, load quarrystep_units.csv, and fit this run. Then press Save report and keep the .txt file.

Target: vacant_45
Validation: the Validation column
Inputs: community, elevator_building, lease_end_month, bedrooms, square_feet, floor, amenity_score, gap_to_market_pct, asked_increase_pct and notice_days
Leave out: concession_months, because it is recorded after the outcome; listing_text, a text column; new_rent_usd, the other target; and every other column of the file, such as the id and date columns.


THE FIRST MESSAGE

Start a new thread with your team's agent, attach the .txt report, and send the message below.

COPY:
Our recommendation is to offer a concession on the units with the highest chance of sitting empty for 45 days. It belongs to the decision on the concession. The attached report is a REG fit of vacant_45, the report the recommendation rests on. I will ask you one question about it at each of five governance gates.


GATE 1, LIVE DATA

COPY:
Call get_rent_index and say which of its values bear on this recommendation, each with its date. What should the community managers or the regional director do if the tool fails?

Then open Execution Details and look at Tool Calls. Check that the agent called get_rent_index and that each value in its answer matches the tool's response.


GATE 2, EVIDENCE

COPY:
Which numbers in the attached report support this recommendation, and how far can the community managers or the regional director trust them? Name the validation rows behind them and any input recorded after the outcome.


GATE 3, FAIRNESS AND PRIVACY

COPY:
Under this recommendation, some tenants and applicants pay different amounts. Could community or elevator_building stand in for income, age or disability, and would acting on it treat people differently in a way fair housing law forbids?


GATE 4, AUTHORITY

COPY:
Which part of this recommendation is yours, and which part does the community managers or the regional director decide? Does any part go beyond the conclusions our brief lists under CONCLUSIONS THE AGENT MAY NOT DRAW?


GATE 5, MONITORING

COPY:
If the community managers or the regional director acts on this recommendation, which number would show that it worked, which number would show that it went wrong, and when should we look again? How would we notice if get_rent_index stopped working?


THEN READ THE ANSWERS

Read the five answers with the box "Reading the answers, gate by gate" on the lecture page, and decide which gate needs the most work. That gate is the main risk of your recommendation, and what would make it pass is your safeguard. Write your team's governance box in your own words.
